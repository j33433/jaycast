The trails

jaycast covers three South Florida MTB parks, each with a different surface and a different response to rain. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo (GFS or ECMWF) and nearby rain gauges when available. Scores are advisory only and are not official park status.

Camp Murphy MTB Trails

Jonathan Dickinson State Park, FL · sandy scrub

Coarse sugar sand under a light canopy. It rides loose and slow when bone-dry and firms up after rain. There is no mud to speak of; this is Florida sand over limestone.

  • Best: about 18 hours after meaningful rain, before the sun dries it back.
  • Worst: a long dry spell, which bottoms out the pack.
  • The trail never officially closes, but dry sand is hard work.

Markham Park Mountain Bike Trails

Weston, FL · dirt and gravel

Dirt and gravel trails that drain slowly. Rain closes them not by ranger decree but because riding wet dirt chews up the surface. The county may also post official closures.

  • Base drain time: 8.5 hours after rain ends, plus 8 hours per additional inch.
  • Rain overnight usually clears by midday; heavy afternoon rain can push reopening to the next day.
  • Short showers under 0.10 in are ignored.

Quiet Waters Park Mountain Bike Trails

Deerfield Beach, FL · mixed hardpack

Firm terrain that stays fast even when dry. A thin layer of loose material over a hard base means puddles and slick roots are the worst of it after rain.

  • The dry baseline score starts near the top.
  • Rain only temporarily degrades the surface; mud clears within about 14 hours.
  • Weather comfort (temperature, wind) matters more here than surface.

How scoring works

Every day gets a star score from 1.0 to 5.0. The model blends surface conditions (pack quality or drainage timing), weather (temperature, wind, sky), and forecast confidence. Each trail is tuned differently: Camp Murphy is a sand-pack model, Markham is a drainage advisory, and Quiet Waters weights comfort weather more because its surface stays rideable.

Frequently asked questions

Is Markham Park rideable after rain?
Markham's dirt and gravel trails drain slowly. jaycast estimates a base of 8.5 hours after rain ends, plus 8 hours per additional inch, capped at 18.5 hours. Heavy afternoon rain can push reopening to the next midday.
When is Camp Murphy best to ride?
Camp Murphy's sugar sand rides best roughly 18 hours after meaningful rain, once the sand firms up and before the sun bakes it back to powder. A long dry spell leaves it loose and slow.
How does jaycast score the trails?
Each trail gets a 1.0 to 5.0 star score from a weather-informed model: surface pack or drainage timing, ride-window rain, temperature, wind, sky, and forecast confidence. Scores are advisory, not official park status.
Do I still need to check official park status?
Yes. jaycast forecasts conditions but is not an official trail status service. Parks can close for reasons weather models cannot see, so check local reports and official notices before heading out.
